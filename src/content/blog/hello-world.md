Hi, I'm Rabin — a software engineer based in Kathmandu. I build and maintain
backend systems with Java and Spring Boot, and I spend a fair amount of time
around Docker, databases, and the Linux command line.

I made this site to have a home on the web that I own, and a place to write up
the things I learn. Expect posts on topics like:

- **Backend engineering** — Spring Boot, APIs, and keeping services maintainable
- **DevOps & systems** — Docker, CI/CD, monitoring, and automation with Bash and Python
- **Databases** — PostgreSQL and MongoDB in real projects
- **Microservices** — lessons from building distributed systems

## Why write at all?

Writing forces me to actually understand a thing before I claim I do. If a
post helps even one other engineer avoid an afternoon of debugging, that's a
good trade.
